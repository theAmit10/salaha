## 2. Description of Service

Salaha is a devotional utility for Apple iOS that provides:

- Daily prayer times based on your location and preferred calculation settings;
- A Qibla compass indicating the direction of prayer;
- Prayer-completion tracking, streaks, and progress views;
- Devotional counters and reminders;
- An optional, voluntary app-blocking feature to support prayer focus;
- An optional Period Mode that pauses reminders and tracking penalties.

The App is designed to work primarily on-device and does not require an account. Certain features require optional system permissions (location, notifications, motion, Screen Time), each of which you may grant or deny. The App remains usable with reduced functionality if you decline optional permissions.
